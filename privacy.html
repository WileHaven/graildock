Privacy Policy

GrailDock ("we", "us") is a collectibles app for scanning, organizing and selling trading cards and other collectibles. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Effective date: November 19, 2026

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), username and display nameTo create and secure your account and sign you in.
Vault items you add: names, categories, grades, prices you enter, notes, and photos you uploadTo show and manage your collection. Photos are stored privately and shown only to you unless you list an item.
Marketplace listings you create: title, description, price and photosTo show your listing to other users of the in-app marketplace. Listings are visible to other signed-in users.
Card photos you scanTo identify a card. Photos are sent to our card-identification service and, where used, the third-party data providers it relies on, and are not used to identify you.
eBay connection (if you choose to connect): your eBay user ID and username, and authorization tokensTo list items on eBay for you. Tokens are stored only on our servers, are never sent to the app, and are deleted when you disconnect.
Subscription status and scan usage countsTo apply your plan's limits.
Support messages you send usTo answer your request.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes your information

We use service providers to run the app, and they handle data only on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions), Expo (app delivery), an email provider (sign-in and password-reset emails), and, when you choose to use them, eBay (listing) and our subscription and payment providers. If you connect eBay, information you tell eBay to publish is governed by eBay's own privacy policy.

Sharing

Other users can see your username, and the content of listings and public items you choose to share. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users and service.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete vault items and listings in the app; the related photos are removed from storage shortly afterwards. You can disconnect eBay at any time from your Profile, which deletes our stored eBay tokens. To delete your account and the data tied to it, contact us at the address below.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to each user's data is restricted at the database level, and private keys and eBay tokens are never stored in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information, or to object to some processing. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
